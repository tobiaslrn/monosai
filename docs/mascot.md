# Sai — character sheet

This document describes the character: what Sai is, what Sai is made of, and how
Sai behaves. It is the authority for the character itself and for nothing else.

It carries a few values — proportion, palette, and the fixed cast of props —
because a character reproduced from prose alone drifts. Everything else is a
rule about what Sai is and what Sai is not.

---

## 1. What Sai is

Sai is the face of a quiet reading application built for people at the stage
where Japanese is still mostly walls. That is the whole brief, and it decides
everything below.

1. **Sai is a reader, not a teacher.** Sai is at the same stage the learner is —
   sitting with a book, watering something small, carrying more books than can
   comfortably be carried. Sai never instructs, corrects, congratulates, or
   supervises. A mascot that grades you is a mascot you resent by week three.
2. **Sai is calm.** Unhurried, absorbed, content in its own company. No urgency,
   no bounce, no excitement.
3. **Sai is decoration.** Sai never carries information, never marks a state, and
   never means success or failure.
4. **Sai is rare.** Scarcity is the reason the character reads as character
   rather than as wallpaper.
5. **Sai is silent.** No speech, no writing, no name spoken aloud.

---

## 2. Silhouette and construction

Sai must be recognisable as a filled black shape at thumbnail size. That test
governs the construction, and it is the reason the sprout exists.

### Locked anatomy

| Element | Rule                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Body    | One soft daikon teardrop: narrow at the top, widest at roughly two-thirds down, flat-bottomed. One mass — no neck, no waist, no separation between head and body.      |
| Height  | Body height ≈ 1.35 × body width at the widest point. Squat, never egg-tall.                                                                                            |
| Sprout  | Two green leaves, centre-top, rising and splaying slightly outward. Always two. Always the same two.                                                                   |
| Arms    | Soft nubs from the body's upper third: no shoulder, no elbow, no hand articulation beyond a mitten curve. Arms may be absent entirely where a pose does not need them. |
| Legs    | Not drawn. Boots attach to the body's lower edge directly.                                                                                                             |
| Boots   | Two yellow rubber boots, rounded toe, short shaft, no sole line, no laces. Both present when standing or walking; hidden by a seated pose.                             |
| Shadow  | One soft ellipse under the body. No cast direction, no contact detail, no second shadow for props.                                                                     |
| Line    | No outline. Form is carried by fill and soft interior shading only.                                                                                                    |
| Texture | A faint paper grain across the whole illustration.                                                                                                                     |

### The sprout is the silhouette

Small, the white body is a blob and the boots disappear. The sprout is the only
part of the silhouette that survives, which is why it is fixed, centred, and
green. It is also the character's name showing. Nothing covers it, crops it, or
replaces it.

### Forbidden

- Fingers, thumbs, feet, ears, a nose, a neck, a tail, hair.
- A second sprout, a flower, a bud, a fruit, or any seasonal variant of the sprout.
- Outlines, hard drop shadows, gradients with visible banding, rim light.
- Accessories worn on the body: hats, scarves, glasses, badges, backpacks. Sai
  holds things; Sai does not wear them.
- Any depiction where the daikon reading is lost. Sai is a vegetable — not a
  ghost, not a bean, not a marshmallow.

---

## 3. Face

The face is four marks and no more.

- **Two eyes.** Solid ovals, slightly taller than wide, set wide apart and low —
  below the vertical midpoint of the head mass. No pupil, no highlight, no
  eyelid, no eyebrow. Closed eyes are a downward arc.
- **One mouth.** A small closed upward curve, narrower than the gap between the
  eyes. An open mouth — a soft rounded shape, never a rectangle, never a visible
  tongue or teeth — belongs only to delight, and delight is rare.
- **Two blushes.** Soft peach ovals outside and slightly below the eyes. Always
  present, at the same relative size. They are not an emotion; they are part of
  the face.

Expression comes from **the body and the props, not the face**. Sai reads by
leaning into a book, is pleased by standing a little straighter, is tired by
sitting down. The face has one setting, and that is the point: it is what makes
Sai calm rather than performative.

Never drawn: sad, crying, angry, worried, confused, shocked, smug, winking,
pleading.

## 4. Colour

Sai is warm paper with a green note.

| Role           | Anchor                                    | Note                                                        |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Body           | `#fefdf6`                                 | Warm near-white, never pure `#ffffff`                       |
| Body shading   | `#faf2e5`                                 | One soft step; a second, deeper fold at `#f6e9d9`           |
| Eyes           | `#313c3d`                                 | Dark slate, never pure black — black reads as a printed dot |
| Blush          | `#ffc8ac`                                 | Soft peach-pink                                             |
| Sprout, leaves | `#70a16b`, lighter face `#8fb87a`         | The same green family as the props                          |
| Ground         | `#feefd3`                                 | Warm, very low contrast                                     |
| Boots          | A warm mid yellow, saturated but not acid | The one high-chroma note in the character                   |

- **The body is never coloured.** No tints for mood, no seasonal palettes.
- **Green is the only identity colour.** The sprout, the books, the watering can
  and the plants sit in one green family.
- **Yellow appears on the boots and nowhere else.**

---

## 5. The cast of props

A fixed, small cast. Every added object is another thing that can be drawn
inconsistently.

| Prop           | Rule                                                                                                      |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Green book     | The reading book. Always the same green, cloth-bound with a plain label panel, held open when read.       |
| Book stack     | Exactly three: green, blue, orange, top to bottom.                                                        |
| Watering can   | Green, spouted, small enough to be carried in one nub.                                                    |
| Sprout in soil | A two-leaf seedling in a small mound — the thing being watered, and a visual rhyme with Sai's own sprout. |
| Potted plant   | Large-leafed, background only, never handled.                                                             |

Never drawn: phones, screens, laptops, pens held in a fist, speech bubbles,
question marks, light bulbs, arrows, sparkles, confetti, hearts, stars, musical
notes. That is the vocabulary of a reaction sticker, and Sai is not one.

---

## 6. Personality

**Temperament.** Unhurried. Absorbed. Slightly over-committed to carrying more
books than can be carried. Content alone. The dominant mode is _concentration_,
not cheer — Sai is usually looking at something, not at you.

**What Sai does.** Reads. Waters. Carries. Waits. Sits down when there is
nothing to do.

**What Sai does not do.** Point. Look at the viewer. Celebrate. Apologise.
Hurry. Explain. Hold a sign.

**How Sai relates to the learner.** As a fellow beginner, not as a guide. Sai is
someone else also working through a stack of books, glimpsed at the edge of
things. While the learner is doing the work, Sai is not in the room.

---

## 7. Silence

Sai does not speak. No speech bubbles, no written words, no thought balloons,
and no lettering anywhere in the illustration. The books have label panels, not
titles.

The name _Sai_ — 菜, greens — belongs to the character, not to the character's
mouth. Sai never introduces itself and is never addressed by name in the picture.

The absence of speech is what lets the calm read as calm rather than as a
personality being performed.

---

## 8. Movement

Sai moves the way Sai stands: slowly, and only as much as the moment needs.

- Motion is small and local. A page turns, a nub lifts, water falls. The body,
  the face, the sprout and the shadow stay put.
- Nothing bobs, bounces, squashes, stretches, or spins. Sai has weight.
- Sai does not react to being looked at, pointed at, or touched. There is no
  glance toward the viewer, ever.
- A loop is long and even. Sai is never in a hurry, so the motion is never quick.

---

## 9. Background

Sai is a daikon sprout: a plain, common, unglamorous vegetable, the kind that
turns up in everything and is nobody's favourite ingredient. That is the joke and
the point — this is about the ordinary daily work of reading, not about mastery.
菜 (_sai_) means greens, and the sprout on the character's head is the name worn
on the body.

Sai's world is one warm room: a window with hills beyond it, a potted plant, a
low shelf of books, a seedling in a pot of soil. **Every scene is that room, or
the ground outside its door.** No classroom, no city, no library, no cloud, no
desk with a computer on it. The room is small on purpose; it is the same room the
learner is reading in.

Sai has no age, no gender, no family, and no companions. There is exactly one
Sai, and Sai is never drawn beside a second one.
