# 11. Risks and Technical Debt

Ordinary tests represent OpenRouter and Anki with controllable fakes, so none of the items below
block CI. They are the checks that need a person, a paid account, or real hardware. A row stays open
until the combination has actually been run, with the real version and date and what was observed —
never what was expected.

## 11.1 Risks

| Priority | Risk | Why it is open | What would close it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **A deployed HTTPS page may not reach AnkiConnect on `127.0.0.1`** | The loopback path was measured from `http://127.0.0.1:4200` and connected to a real collection. Chrome gates some private-network behaviour on secure contexts, and the deployed HTTPS origin has never been observed doing it | One run from the deployed Pages URL against a real Anki Desktop with the add-on installed. See [ADR 0017](../decisions/0017-anki-connect-origin-policy.md) |
| 2 | **The Android bridge path has never met a real bridge** | It is exercised against a fake provider and the shared contract test only. No Android hardware or emulator with a bridge app was available | One vocabulary refresh on a physical Android device with an AnkiConnect-compatible bridge |
| 3 | **No real Anki export has been imported** | Fixture coverage is thorough and covers the schema variance Anki has shipped, but every fixture is synthetic and generated by a build script | One `.apkg` and one `.colpkg` exported from a current Anki Desktop, run through the importer |
| 4 | **No live OpenRouter text model has been evaluated** | Every hard constraint is covered locally against fixtures. Comparative narrative quality across model families has not been judged, because no live key was available | The blind manual run over the fixed prompt corpus, with three configurable model families |
| 5 | **No real audio has come back from a provider** | The request contract, the rejection fallback, the context cap, and the context-sensitive cache keys are fixture-tested. Naturalness and contextual intonation are not | A live run against a compatible TTS model and voice, plus a human A/B review |
| 6 | **Android background audio is unverified on hardware** | Media Session metadata, sentence navigation, seeking, and one continuous media resource — including one that grows while a generation run fills it in — are covered by automated tests. Screen-locked playback, app switching, notification controls, headset buttons, and whether a stall at the generation frontier survives a locked screen are not | One session on a physical Android device with the screen locked, over a reading started before its audio was finished. See [ADR 0039](../decisions/0039-continuous-android-audio.md) and [ADR 0045](../decisions/0045-a-reading-is-extended-while-it-is-generated.md) |
| 7 | **The PWA has never been installed from the deployed URL** | Installability, the base path, and offline reload pass against a locally served production build, which is the closest check possible before a deploy | A real Chrome install prompt from `https://<owner>.github.io/monosai/`, and the browser's own installability judgement |
| 8 | **No manual screen-reader pass has been done** | Automated axe scans, the token-contrast check, and the reduced-motion guards run in CI. They are not a substitute for a screen reader | One pass with NVDA, VoiceOver, or an equivalent, over the library, the reader, and generation |

Risks 1 to 3 sit at the top because they concern the vocabulary source. If any of them fails in the
field, word marking is wrong, and quality goal 2 — being honest about what the learner knows — fails
with it.

## 11.2 Technical debt

| Item | Cost today | Suggested action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The reader page still pushes its stores with effects | `features/reader/reader-page.component.ts` is now composition and template: 1,125 lines, most of them template and styles, with 15 injected dependencies and 5 effects. Scrolling, measurement, and placement moved into `ReaderScrollService`, `ReaderGeometryService`, `ReaderWordPreviewService`, and the `mnDockedPlayer` and `mnReaderParagraphWindow` directives, each tested on its own ([chapter 5](05-building-block-view.md)). What remain are the five effects that tell the stores what happened: open the reading, tell the aids store which window is mounted, re-read summaries while a job runs, prepare playback, and release a wait for clips that will never come. The page still cannot be tested without mounting all of it | The five stay until their causes have an owner: `SentenceAidsStore` would have to follow the reader's mounted paragraphs itself, the application-wide playback store would have to watch one reading's audio job, which is the reader's to report, and the job stores do not say when they wrote |
| The largest remaining files are a page, a settings section, and two stores | After the reader page (1,125 lines), `features/settings/models-section.component.ts` (877), `application/enrichment/translation-job.store.ts` (802), and `application/generation/generation.store.ts` (799) are the largest. The reader's player component was among them at 1,161 lines — roughly 210 of template, 300 of styles, and 540 of class — and is 516 now: what its controls are moved to `reading-player-view.ts` and what they say to `reading-player-copy.ts`, both plain functions tested with literals, and the scrubber and the generation ring became `ReadingTrackComponent` and `GenerationRingComponent` ([chapter 5](05-building-block-view.md)). Playback was split behind `AudioPlaybackStore` into clips, transport state, clip sequencing, navigation, resumption, and maintenance, which took the store from 1,554 to 279 lines, and the story repair loop left `GenerationStore` for `StoryRepairService` | Look for the reader page's seams — what a component measures, and what it commands — before adding to `models-section.component.ts`, and for the job stores' shared seams before adding to `translation-job.store.ts` |
| State changes still drive work through effects | 27 `effect()` calls remain: 5 in the reader page and 22 elsewhere. State resets and copies are `linkedSignal`, the Library and vocabulary lists' windowing is the shared virtual-list directive's two effects, and the reader's DOM effects moved into its geometry and paragraph-window units. The asynchronous reloads that fired when another store's state changed are `resource()`: the history after a package import in `reading-level-page.component.ts` and `source-page.component.ts`, the standings in `source-list.component.ts`, the model catalogue in `models-section.component.ts`, and the stored aids in `sentence-aids.store.ts`. A refresh needs no trigger, because `AnkiConnectionStore.confirm` reloads the history itself. Nothing that reloads data is left. The effects that start work or push state into a store are the reader page's five, the lane pump in `preparation.store.ts`, the saved-story report in `generation-jobs.store.ts`, and the notices offered after a navigation in `app-shell.component.ts`, each commented where it stands with why it is an effect, plus `jobs.watch` in the generate page, the catalogue parameters pushed into `TtsStore`, and the shared-file hand-off in `reading-level-page.component.ts` | The reader page's five go with the owners named above. The others are each the one place their state meets the outside world, kept with `untracked()` where they write back; turn one into an explicit call or a `resource()` if it starts to loop. [Chapter 8](08-crosscutting-concepts.md) holds the rule for new code |
| Two records share the ADR number `0028` | A reference to "ADR 0028" is ambiguous | Leave the files as they are. Both are linked from code comments and other records, so renumbering would break more than it fixes. [Chapter 9](09-architecture-decisions.md) lists both by title |
| The player still carries a WAV concatenation path | Speech is stored compressed, but a browser without an audio encoder still writes `audio/wav`, and a learner whose background re-encode never ran or was stopped still holds WAV rows. `combineWaveClips` and the WAV branch of `playSequence` exist for them | Remove both once `audio/wav` can no longer be produced — conditioned on that, not on a date. See [ADR 0070](../decisions/0070-gemini-speech-is-stored-compressed.md) |
